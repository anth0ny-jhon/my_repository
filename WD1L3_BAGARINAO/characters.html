01 / THE HEROES

01

Sonic the Hedgehog

Sonic is a confident, energetic blue hedgehog famous for his incredible speed. He enjoys adventure and often acts before thinking, especially when someone needs his help. Despite his playful and sometimes impatient personality, Sonic is brave and determined. He takes the lead when Planet Freedom faces danger and refuses to abandon his friends.

Japanese Voice Actor: Masami Kikuchi

02

Miles "Tails" Prower

Tails is a young, intelligent fox with two distinctive tails that allow him to fly. He is Sonic's loyal friend and frequently accompanies him on dangerous adventures. Although he can sometimes be nervous, Tails makes up for it with his intelligence, creativity, and technical abilities.

Japanese Voice Actor: Hekiru Shiina
CHARACTER
JAPANESE VOICE ACTOR
03

Knuckles the Echidna

Knuckles is a powerful red echidna known for his incredible strength and determination. He is serious, independent, and sometimes stubborn, but he has a strong sense of duty. He joins Sonic and Tails in their mission to stop Robotnik's dangerous plans.

Japanese Voice Actor: Yasunori Matsumoto

01

Dr. Ivo Robotnik

Dr. Robotnik is a brilliant but arrogant scientist who rules the Land of Darkness. He manipulates Sonic into helping him stop a supposed disaster involving the Robot Generator, hiding his own motives behind his desperate requests for assistance.

Japanese Voice Actor: Junpei Takiguchi
CHARACTER
JAPANESE VOICE ACTOR
02

Metal Sonic

Metal Sonic is a robotic duplicate of Sonic designed to imitate his appearance and speed. Created as a powerful mechanical threat, he becomes one of the central dangers that Sonic and his friends must confront.

Japanese Voice Actor: Masami Kikuchi
